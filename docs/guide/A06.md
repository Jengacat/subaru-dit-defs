# A06. Closed loop, learning and open-loop gating

Detail behind C06. LF75404S; decoded from code, untested on a car.

## A06.1 Why the ECU is in open loop

Checked in this order every pass; the first match wins. Only state 1 is closed loop.

| State | Condition (stock) | Meaning for a tuner |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A sensor or monitor fault flag | Fix the fault, not the fuel tables |
| 3, 6 | Status flags not decoded (3 adds a hold time by rpm) | Rare |
| 7, 11 | Out of reach / disabled | Never in stock |
| 9 | Target richer than **Closed Loop Minimum Target**, or primary open-loop, additional, warm-up or after-start enrichment active | *Open loop for enrichment*: the normal WOT case |
| 10 | Not running, or coolant at or below 5 °F (-15 °C) | Cold / stopped |
| 1 | None of the above | Closed loop |

## A06.2 The closed-loop target

1. One of the four Closed Loop Target tables, by TGV state and estimated EGR flow (0.67 g/s or more = EGR Active).
2. Plus **Closed Loop Target Cold-Start Trim (TGVs Open)** / (TGVs Closed), richened by **Low-DAM Enrichment**.
3. The richer of that and the lean limit, **Closed Loop Target Lean Limit (TGVs Open, Normal Mode)** and siblings
   (Normal Mode tables in combustion mode 4, Other Modes otherwise); then a small undecoded offset and a clamp.
4. Replaced by **Closed Loop Target (Catalyst Monitor Active)** during the catalyst test, or
   by **Closed Loop Target Override** (+ Low-DAM Enrichment) under an unidentified flag.

Logged as L15; J20 is an earlier stage and only covers λ 0.96 to 1.17.

## A06.3 The controller

Step = (target − measured λ, shaped) × **Closed Loop Integral Gain (Positive Error)** / (Negative Error) × a coolant
factor. The sensor reading goes through **A/F Sensor Scaling (Wide Range)** and its near-stoich twin. The correction
(L16) reaches the injector as the short-term trim term, inside limits by coolant (link read in part, inferred).

| Pedal | Coolant | Shaping curves |
|---|---|---|
| Released | any | **Closed Loop Integral Error Shaping (Idle, Negative Error)** / (Idle, Positive Error) |
| Pressed | below 122 °F (50 °C) | **Closed Loop Integral Error Shaping (Cold, Negative Error)** / (Cold, Positive Error) |
| Pressed | 122 °F and above | **Closed Loop Integral Error Shaping (Warm, Negative Error)** / (Warm, Positive Error) |

## A06.4 Long-term learning

*Learns only when all hold:* closed loop; coolant 167 °F (75 °C) or more; error about 3.6 % or less; load above an
error-dependent minimum; per-cylinder transient fuel within ±0.3 %; a settle count passed; no enrichment of any kind;
combustion mode 4; several status flags clear. Trims that never move mean one of these fails.

*Update:* average the correction over **Closed Loop Learning Averaging Passes** (112 at low rpm/load down to 24);
beyond ±0.8 % step the cell by **Closed Loop Learning Step**; clamp at ±115 counts (about ±45 %). Cell = load range
(**LTFT Load Range Boundary 1** to 4) + 5 if rpm is at or above **LTFT RPM Range Boundary**.

*Apply:* the cell is filtered, scaled by **LTFT Coolant Scaling (Positive)** / (Negative), limited
by **LTFT Floor (Restricted)** in some states, and becomes the LTFT (128 = 0 %, 0.39 %/count), in open loop too.

## A06.5 Open-loop gating

* **Primary Open Loop Fueling** is on while the exhaust temperature estimate is at or above cal 0x18DD4 (39,322 raw,
  hysteresis 0x18DD6 = 2,185; not in the definition). A second path has a threshold out of reach.
* The TGV/AVCS group counts only once the load timer reaches its delay, and only at 5 counts or
  more. **Exhaust Temperature Estimate 2 Map (No Enrichment)** reaching its threshold zeroes the delay.
* Enrichment rises at an rpm-dependent rate and drops at once.
* Commanded λ in open loop = 256 / (256 + warm-up + primary open-loop + additional enrichment), as the OBD PID shows.
